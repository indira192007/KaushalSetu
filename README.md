# KaushalSetu
### Connecting Skills. Building Trust. Creating Opportunities.

## About the Project
KaushalSetu is a digital skill verification platform designed for informal construction and home-service workers.

## Problem Statement
Many electricians, plumbers, painters and carpenters have years of practical experience but struggle to prove their skills when searching for employment.

## Our Solution
KaushalSetu allows workers to upload videos and images demonstrating their practical skills. Authorized verifiers assess their abilities and provide digital skill profiles with QR codes.

Employers and contractors can scan these QR codes to check verified skill records.

## Key Features
- Worker registration
- Skill demonstration through videos and images
- Authorized skill assessment
- Digital skill profiles
- QR-code-based verification
- Employer access to verified records

## Target Users
- Electricians
- Plumbers
- Painters
- Carpenters
- Construction workers
- Contractors and employers

## Social Impact
Making practical skills visible, verifiable and accessible to employment opportunities.
